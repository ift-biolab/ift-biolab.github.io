## What is it?

A Cecotec domestic microwave oven recorded in the BioLab purchase inventory.

**Reference:** MCRW-20L-001 · **Model:** ProClean 3010 (manufacturer reference 01522) · **Supplier:** Labbox.

**Manufacturer / brand:** Cecotec.

## Main capabilities

Microwave heating with mechanical controls. The manufacturer describes heating food and beverages, not laboratory processing.

## Main specifications

Manufacturer manual for power, supply and capacity; supplier technical datasheet PDS-1399, revised **25 September 2025**, for dimensions. The manufacturer page is undated and disagrees on dimensions.

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| Capacity | 20 L |
| Microwave output / electrical consumption | 700 W / 1,150 W (manufacturer manual) |
| Supply | 230 V AC, 50 Hz |
| Controls | Six power levels; timer up to 30 minutes |
| External dimensions, supplier datasheet | 445 × 355 × 255 mm |
| Internal dimensions, supplier datasheet | 285 × 277 × 175 mm |
