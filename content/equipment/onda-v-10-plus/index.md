## What is it?

A visible-light spectrophotometer for measurements in cuvettes.

**Reference:** SPTR-V01-001 · **Model:** V-10 PLUS · **Supplier:** Labbox.

**Manufacturer / brand:** ONDA; Labbox Labware is the CE declaring entity (OEM not identified).

## Main capabilities

Absorbance, transmittance and concentration using a standard curve or a factor. Software-related functions do not establish a validated BioLab assay.
