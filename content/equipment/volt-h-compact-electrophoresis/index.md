## What is it?

A horizontal gel electrophoresis system with an integrated power supply.

**Reference:** ELFH-50V-001 · **Model:** H-Compact · **Supplier:** Labbox.

**Manufacturer / brand:** VOLT (brand; OEM not identified).

Horizontal electrophoresis with adjustable voltage/current and timed or continuous operation.
