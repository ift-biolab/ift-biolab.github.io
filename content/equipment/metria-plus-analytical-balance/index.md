## What is it?

An analytical balance for measuring mass, with internal calibration.

**Reference:** BAMP-220-001 · **Model:** PLUS, 220 g, internal calibration · **Supplier:** Labbox.

**Manufacturer / brand:** METRIA (brand; OEM not identified).

## Main capabilities

Weighing with a 0.0001 g display increment; selectable mass units.
